# E002 — Execution sensitivity: specification (frozen 2026-10-20, before any simulation on market data; amended the same day after the adversarial engine review, still before any simulation on market data — section 10 takes precedence where it differs)

**Question (RESEARCH_SPEC H2).** Do simulated passive market-making outcomes and policy rankings change materially across
plausible queue/fill, latency and fee assumptions on the same historical market paths?
**Status label of every output:** *modeled historical replay*. Our orders never existed; fills are inferred under the
stated assumptions; no market impact is modeled or claimed.
**Machine-readable parameters:** `configs/e002_v1.toml`. **Evidence for assumptions:** `E002_assumptions_evidence.md`.

## 1. Sample and episodes
- Binance spot SOLUSDT; discovery sessions only (10 train + 5 validation first-of-month days). The 5 test sessions stay sealed until the Stage 4 close-out.
- An **episode** is one accepted joint validity window of at least 600 s (Acceptance Test 001 run of record `50807dc`). Each episode starts flat with no orders; at its end all orders are cancelled at the last valid state (data validity ends there; no fill is inferred beyond it) and inventory is valued twice: marked to mid, and liquidation-adjusted (a market order walking the visible top-10 levels, plus taker fee; any unliquidated remainder is flagged).
- Episode ends are data boundaries, not decisions. Selection by future price path (windows end when the touch leaves the proven region) is inherited from Test 001 and disclosed as in E001.

## 2. Information and timing
- The participant sees a book state at the Tardis recorder's receipt time plus a market-data delay, and learns of its own fills after the acknowledgement latency. Orders and cancels reach the exchange after the entry latency; an order is never fillable before arrival; a cancelled order remains fillable until the cancel arrives (ties at the same microsecond go to the cancel).
- Exchange-side events are ordered: trade → depth state → order arrival/cancel → participant decision. An order arriving at time t interacts only with trades strictly after t.
- Latency brackets (one-way entry = acknowledgement = extra market-data delay): **Tokyo-hosted** 2 ms / 2 ms / 0 ms (fast order entry, but decisions still run on the 100 ms depth batches), **regional** 30 / 30 / 30 ms, **distant** 115 / 115 / 115 ms.

## 3. Orders, queue position and fills
- Post-only limit orders (`LIMIT_MAKER`): rejected at arrival if they would cross the current book. One working order per side; prices on the 0.01 tick, sizes rounded down to the 0.001 lot, minimum notional 5 USDT; at most 100 new orders per 10 s and 200,000 per UTC day (counted conservatively: every new order counts, fills do not decrement).
- Queue position (price-time priority; L2 gives level sizes, not positions): on arrival the order joins the back of its level (queue ahead = visible level size minus executions at that price since the last state; unknown if the price lies beyond the visible depth, then treated as infinitely far back). Executions at the order's price consume the queue ahead first, then fill the order (partial fills allowed). A trade at a worse price for the order's side implies the level was exhausted: the order fills up to that trade's size. Additions to a level join behind the order.
- **Fill models** (the only difference is how net cancellations are attributed, plus crossing fills in the optimistic model):

| Model | Net non-trade decrease of the level (N < 0) | Book moves to/through the order's price | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| conservative | no credit (cancellations assumed behind) | no fill unless a trade goes through | lower bracket (hftbacktest `RiskAdverseQueueModel`) |
| intermediate | credited pro rata: ahead −= abs(N) · ahead / (level − executions) | as conservative | central (identity-function `ProbQueueModel`) |
| optimistic | credited ahead first: ahead −= abs(N) | fills up to the opposite visible size at or through the price | upper bracket |
| naive (reference only) | queue ignored: any trade at the price fills | as optimistic | shows touch-fill optimism; never reported as a result |

  In every model the queue ahead never exceeds the visible level. Within-batch (100 ms) gross flows are unobservable; only net changes are used.

## 4. Policies (fixed a priori; nothing is tuned)
- **No-trade** baseline (zero P&L, zero risk).
- **P0 fixed:** quote both sides at the visible touch, 1 SOL each; a side is posted only if a full fill keeps acknowledged inventory within ±10 SOL; requote (cancel-replace) whenever the visible touch price changes.
- **P1 inventory withdrawal:** as P0, but withdraw the side that would add to inventory once |inventory| ≥ 3 SOL. (With a one-tick spread, withdrawal is the available skew.)

## 5. Fees (accounting overlays; policies ignore fees, so fills are identical across schedules)

| Schedule | Maker / taker | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **vip0** | 10.0 / 10.0 bp | **default — the only attainable schedule for this project** |
| vip0_bnb | 7.5 / 7.5 bp | hypothetical (requires holding BNB) |
| vip9 | 1.1 / 2.3 bp | hypothetical (≥ 4B USD 30-day volume) |
| lp_tier1 | 0.0 / 2.3 bp | hypothetical (≥ 25M USD weekly maker volume); taker rate assumed equal to VIP9 |
| lp_tier4 | −0.8 / 2.3 bp | hypothetical upper bound (≥ 1% of exchange maker volume); taker rate assumed equal to VIP9 |

Gross (pre-fee) results are reported only as a decomposition, never as a fee scenario.

## 6. Accounting conventions
- Cash in USDT, inventory in SOL; every fee booked in USDT at the fill price (fee = rate × notional; rebates negative). Binance takes buy-side fees in SOL without BNB; the difference is second order (rate × later price change) and is disclosed.
- Marked wealth W = cash + inventory × mid (mid of the latest exchange-clock state). Identity enforced at run time and in tests: W_end = Σ fill edge vs mid at the fill + Σ inventory × Δmid − fees.
- Terminal valuation: marked, and liquidation-adjusted (marked − walking cost − taker fee). Results use the liquidation-adjusted value unless stated.
- No double counting: spread capture appears only as fill edge; markouts of own fills are diagnostics computed from the same fills.

## 7. Outputs and analysis (pre-registered)
- Per (session, policy, fill model, latency, fee schedule): net P&L (USDT; bp of traded notional; USDT per valid hour), gross P&L, fees, liquidation cost, fills, traded notional, fill rate, orders sent/rejected/rate-limited, max and time-weighted |inventory|, maximum drawdown of marked wealth, own-fill markouts and rs/2 at 1 s and 30 s.
- Inference: sessions are the unit; mean of session means with 10,000-draw session-bootstrap 95% intervals.
- **H2 decision rule** (default fee schedule): H2 holds on discovery sessions if (a) the session-mean net outcome of P0 or P1 has intervals excluding zero on opposite sides under two fill-model × latency scenarios, or (b) the paired P1 − P0 difference has intervals excluding zero in opposite directions under two scenarios; sensitivity is called **material** if the range of session-mean net bp across the three primary fill models at fixed latency is ≥ 1.0 bp (about one tick). Everything else is descriptive. The naive model is excluded from the decision.
- Ranking statements compare policies on the same sessions, fill model, latency and fees only.

## 8. Acceptance criteria before any E002 run on market data
1. Toy-case tests pass for: queue consumption and partial fills; each model's cancellation attribution; queue cap; trade-through fills; crossing fills (optimistic only); entry/cancel/ack latency; post-only rejection; lot, notional and both rate limits; inventory limits; look-ahead-safe decisions; unknown-queue handling; accounting identity; liquidation walk; no-trade baseline.
2. This specification, `configs/e002_v1.toml` and the assumptions evidence are committed before the first run, and the engine has passed an adversarial review with no unresolved critical or major finding.
3. The runner asserts per episode: accounting identity within 1e-6 USDT; no fill outside the episode or before an order's arrival; fills only at the order's price or after a trade at a worse price (or a crossing book, optimistic model); order counts within limits.
4. Runs are made from a clean committed tree; outputs record git commit, config hashes and input hashes; test sessions are refused by the code.

## 9. Known limitations
No market impact (our 1-SOL orders do not alter historical trades or book; the replay cannot react to them). Queue position is modeled, not observed (public spot trades carry no order ids since 2024-06). Hidden iceberg quantity and intra-100 ms gross flows are invisible. Latency brackets are calibrations, not measurements of this participant. Fees for 2025-03 → 2025-10 are inferred from identical values on both sides of that interval. First-of-month sampling; 15 discovery sessions.

## 10. Amendments before any run (2026-10-20)
Made after an adversarial review of the engine, runner and analysis (confirmed defects listed in `DECISIONS.md`, 2026-10-20). **No E002 simulation had been run on market data; no E002 outcome had been computed or seen.** These amendments take precedence over sections 1–8 where they differ.

1. **Participant view (look-ahead fix).** Decisions see the latest received state, inventory from *acknowledged* fills only, and their own working orders as last acknowledged. Exchange-side fills reach the participant after the acknowledgement latency; post-only rejections after the same latency (the order counts as working until then; the participant re-decides at the next received state, not immediately on the same stale state). No decision is taken after the episode end. The ±10 SOL limit applies to acknowledged inventory; exchange-side inventory can exceed it by fills in flight, and the realized maximum is reported.
2. **Order retention.** A working order at the price the policy wants is kept, partially filled or not (queue position retained; no top-up to full size). "Requote whenever the touch price changes" means cancel-replace on a price change only.
3. **Arrival queue.** Executions at the order's price between the last state and the arrival reduce the queue ahead once; they are not re-attributed as cancellations at the next state (a double credit in the reviewed code).
4. **Crossing fills** (optimistic and naive models): at most once per crossing episode; the order must see an uncrossed state before it can fill this way again.
5. **Liquidation shortfall.** Inventory beyond the visible top-10 levels is valued at the deepest visible price plus the taker fee and flagged (`liquidation_shortfall`, `episodes_with_shortfall`); this is not a valid execution price and every occurrence is reported.
6. **Drawdown.** Maximum drawdown is computed per fee schedule on the session wealth path: sampled at every depth state, liquidation-adjusted at each episode end, carried across the session's episodes (each episode restarts flat from the previous liquidation-adjusted wealth). Order-rate counters (10 s window and daily count) also carry across episodes.
7. **Own-fill diagnostics.** `own_edge_bp` = notional-weighted sign × (mid before the fill − price), in bp of price (the edge term). `own_markout_{h}s_bp` = sign × (mid at T + h − price) in bp of price — the passive mark-to-mid, i.e. E001's rs(h)/2 measured on our own fills. Fills whose T + h lies beyond their episode end are excluded and the number included is reported.
8. **Sessions without fills.** The bp outcome is undefined for a session without fills; it is reported, never imputed (USDT per valid hour is reported for every session). A scenario enters H2 conditions (a) and (b) only if its outcome is defined in at least 12 of the 15 discovery sessions; others are reported as having insufficient fills.
9. **Materiality.** Replaces the range of point estimates: sensitivity is **material** if, for some policy and latency, the paired session difference between two primary fill models has |mean| ≥ 1.0 bp and a 95% session-bootstrap interval excluding zero (sessions where both are defined; ≥ 12). The range of point estimates is kept as a descriptive figure.
10. **Confirmation on the test sessions (Stage 4 close-out only).** The 5 test sessions are run once with this frozen code and configuration. H2 is *confirmed* if at least one discovery-stage pair that satisfied (a) or (b) shows, on the test sessions, both scenario means with the same signs as on discovery; materiality is confirmed if a discovery-material model difference has the same sign and |mean| ≥ 1.0 bp on the test sessions. Five sessions are too few for interval-based decisions, so these are point-estimate sign checks and are reported as such. If H2 is not supported on discovery, the test sessions only report the same tables; no confirmation claim is made.
11. **Labels and assumptions.** The fastest bracket is "Tokyo-hosted": low entry latency does not make the 100 ms depth stream faster. LP-programme taker rates are assumed equal to the VIP9 taker (2.3 bp). Fees for 2025-03 → 2025-10 are confirmed by inference (identical published values on both sides of the interval), not by a dated snapshot.
12. **Runner assertions** (extends 8.3): crossing fills only in the optimistic models; every fill inside its episode; order counts within the daily limit and carried across episodes; state validity layer taken from the configuration; analysis refuses outputs containing non-discovery sessions, from a dirty tree, or whose configuration or specification changed after the run.
13. **Second review (2026-10-21; record `docs/audits/2026-10-21_e002_rereview.md`; still no E002 outcome computed or seen).** All eleven earlier fixes were checked as correct. One major finding was fixed: P1's ±3 SOL threshold is compared with a 1e-9 tolerance, since inventory is a float sum of fills. Minor findings fixed before any run:
    - every fill is rounded down to the 0.001 lot, which removes float residue and sub-lot pro-rata fills;
    - an order arriving after the episode end is neither judged against the stale book nor counted as quoted;
    - **fill rate** (pre-registered in section 7) = filled quantity / quantity of orders that reached the book;
    - `tw_abs_inventory_s` is the integral of |inventory| (SOL·s), and `tw_abs_inventory_avg` is its time average;
    - blocks by the daily cap are counted separately (`rate_limited_daily`);
    - provenance is captured before the simulation and re-checked at the end; the run refuses an uncommitted specification or assumptions file and an existing `results/e002`; the metadata is written last with hashes of both tables;
    - the analysis also pins `research_v1.toml`, the code fingerprint and the net-table hash, writes valid JSON (NaN → null), and sorts its input so intervals do not depend on row order.

    Clarifications:
    - with reactive policies the fill models are not per-session bounds, because paths diverge after the first fill; "lower/upper bracket" describes assumptions, not guaranteed orderings of outcomes;
    - the close-out run of 10.10 needs a dedicated, separately guarded entry point, added and logged in `DECISIONS.md` before the close-out; test-session means use defined sessions only.

    Remaining minors (untested guards, a fill notice re-quoting a just-rejected price on the same stale state, a tautological order-count assertion, NaN `net_per_hour` for a session without episodes) are listed in the record and do not change the pre-registered quantities.
