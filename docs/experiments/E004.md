# E004 — Information robustness: specification (FROZEN 2026-10-23 before any E004 outcome was computed; revised after two independent review rounds)

**Question (RESEARCH_SPEC H4).** When the participant's market information degrades — delayed, withheld or noisy — while
the market, the exchange and the truth do not, does an *uncertainty-aware* quoting rule outperform an otherwise identical
one that treats degraded inputs as fresh?

**Operationalisation (to be logged in `DECISIONS.md` at freeze).** H4 is tested where the answer is empirical and free
of the activity confound found in E002, E003 and A1 (at the attainable fee, any rule that trades less "wins"):
- **Primary.** At the policy level, inside a **single** naive run, are fills during *staleness-timed* windows worse than
  fills during *duty-matched, randomly timed* windows? This is fee-free and involves no path divergence. Both arms
  share the same run and matched withdrawal *duty*; fills and turnover are not matched, and per-notional quality is
  compared.
- **Secondary.** At the forecast level. The first review showed that its outcome is largely fixed by the choice of target
  together with imbalance's known short memory. It is therefore reported against pre-registered projections, not as a
  discovery.

The designs come from an independent three-design panel with two judges (record: `docs/audits/2026-10-23_e004_design_panel.md`).
This specification synthesises them and was revised after an adversarial review (record:
`docs/audits/2026-10-23_e004_spec_review.md`).

## 2. Degradations (participant information only)

**Invariant.** The exchange-side event stream is bit-identical in every condition: true states, all trades, queue
estimates, post-only checks and fills. Targets and evaluation mids come only from the reference path. Withheld records
are kept for evaluation and for the exchange side only. Order entry and the user-data stream (acknowledgements,
rejections) are assumed unaffected, because they use separate connections; this is a stated limitation.

**One clock.** A record's *delivery time* d is on the recorder clock: its t_recv, or its release time under D3 or a resync.
The participant can act on it at d + md_delay. Schedules are drawn on this clock over the whole UTC day, independently of
episodes.

| Cell | Mechanism | Parameters | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D0** | none (reference) | — | identity, reproduction, do-no-harm |
| **D1-E** | connection drop, *withhold*: book states **and** trades delivered in a stall are lost; resync (full top-10 book) on resume | Poisson onsets 120/h; durations drawn uniformly from the 6 discovery reconnect gaps (0.949–1.924 s); overlaps merged (≈4.5% stalled) | **primary (policy level)** |
| **D1-S** | book-feed stall, *withhold*: depth states are lost; trades continue (separate stream); resync on resume | alternating renewal: up ~ Exp(15 s), stall ~ Exp(3 s), capped at 30 s (≈16.7% stalled) | secondary (forecast; policy descriptive) |
| D1-S1 / D1-S3 | as D1-S | up Exp(60 s) / stall Exp(1 s) (≈1.6%); up Exp(10 s) / stall Exp(6 s) (≈37.5%) | dose-response (descriptive) |
| D3 | *delay*: D1-S's schedule, but records are delivered in order at the stall end (catch-up burst) | as D1-S | secondary: late vs lost information |
| D2 | trade-feed outage, *withhold* | up Exp(30 s), outage Exp(5 s) (≈14%) | negative control (flow coefficients ≈ 0) |
| D4 | size *noise*: displayed level sizes × exp(σε − σ²/2), ε ~ N(0,1) iid per level and state, lot-rounded, at least 1 lot; prices untouched | regime renewal: clean Exp(900 s) / noisy Exp(300 s) (25% noisy), σ = 0.5 | secondary; low realism |

The D1-E rate is about 7,000× the discovery reconnect rate (6 in about 360 h). Only the durations are empirical; the
rate is a stress level.

**Resync.** On resume, the record delivered is the last reference state with t_recv ≤ stall end, delivered at d = the
stall end. d ≥ t_recv is asserted for every delivered record.

**Indicators the participant observes** (computed from delivered data only):
- **D1, D3: age.** α(t) = t − t_exch(j), where j is the last state with d(j) + md_delay ≤ t. It uses Binance's event time
  E and assumes clock sync. Natural gaps between depth updates also raise α. These are not trade-free: trades continue
  at about half the usual rate.
- **D4: feed-quality flag**, 1 in the noisy regime.
- **D2: trade-gap flag**, 1 if the trade ids *delivered* by t are non-consecutive anywhere in the 10 s flow window.
  Natural recorder gaps also trigger it; its D0 trigger share is reported.

**Seeds.** `numpy.random.SeedSequence(entropy=20261023, spawn_key=(cell_code, yyyymmdd, role, replicate))` with PCG64.
Integer cell codes are listed in the configuration.

| Role | Use |
|---|---|
| 0 | training degradation |
| 1 | evaluation degradation |
| 2 | placebo-indicator training draw |
| 3 | placebo-indicator evaluation draw |
| 4 | placebo-window offsets |

- **Replicates:** 3 evaluation replicates and 1 training draw. Evaluation seeds never train (asserted).
- **Schedule hashes.** The date-only generator produces the renewal and Poisson schedules and the D4 regimes for all
  20 days, without reading any data. The hash is SHA-256 of the little-endian int64 microsecond endpoints after merging
  (rounded toward −∞), per cell, role, replicate and day.
- **Noise draws** come from `SeedSequence(entropy, spawn_key=(cell_code, yyyymmdd, role, replicate, 1))`, indexed by
  state, and are not covered by the schedule hashes.

## 3. Forecast models (secondary; walk-forward; linear)

**Rows.** E003's eligible evaluation rows k of s6…s15, at decision time t_k = t_recv(S_k) + 30 ms.

**Delivered state.** j(k) is the last state with d(j) + md_delay ≤ t_k, ties broken by index ≤ k. Under D0, j(k) = k
(asserted), and NV equals REF bitwise.

**Degraded features.** x̃_k are E003's 8 regressors, computed by the unchanged `episode_features` on the *delivered*
arrays and read at j(k):
- delayed delivery times under D3;
- noisy sizes under D4;
- surviving trades under D1-E and D2.

Features are frozen at j(k), so trades that keep flowing during a D1-S stall do not enter. D1-S and D1-E therefore differ
at the forecast level only through trade loss at resync. Rows with no delivered state yet go to the oldest age bucket with
f = 0, and are counted.

**Targets (both reported).**
- **y_k** = E003's y_5s: the move from the true current mid mid(S_k). That mid is hidden from the participant during a
  stall.
- **y_view** = 1e4 · log(mid_ref(t_k + 5 s) / mid(S_{j(k)})): the move from the mid the participant prices off, which is
  what quotes face.

Under D0 the two are identical.

**Models:**

| Model | Definition |
|---|---|
| REF | E003 M2 on clean features (benchmark only) |
| NV | M2 coefficients on x̃_k |
| RF-λ | one λ, fitted on degraded training rows |
| UA-λ | λ_b within buckets of the actual indicator |
| PL-λ | as UA-λ, with the placebo indicator (independent draw of the same law) |
| UA-β | M2 refitted per bucket (descriptive) |

- λ is fitted by session-weighted least squares without intercept, separately for each target, on degraded training rows
  (training seeds), unconstrained.
- f_NV on training rows uses the s_k model (fitted on s1…s_{k−1}). These rows are partly in-sample for that model, so
  λ in the fresh bucket is ≈ 1 by construction (disclosed).

**Buckets.**
- Age: [0, 0.65 s], (0.65, 1], (1, 3], (3, 10], > 10 s.
- Flag: 0/1.
- A bucket with fewer than 5,000 training rows merges into its neighbour toward the fresh end; the freshest bucket
  merges upward.

**τ = 650 ms.** The rule was set first at an a-priori floor of 350 ms, then amended to the data rule before any outcome:
max(350 ms, the 99.9th percentile of reference-path inter-update gaps rounded up to 50 ms). That percentile is 600.3 ms,
from message timing on the discovery sessions only (`tools/e004_tau_timing.py`). On the delivery-decision clock after
warm-up, the reference path spends 0.148% of its time with age ≥ τ (0.003–0.81% per session).

## 4. Policies

**Common rules.** Bases B ∈ {P0, P3[M2]} with E003's common rules:
- 1 SOL; ±10 SOL limit on acknowledged inventory;
- post-only at the touch of the **view** state;
- pacing 1.15 orders/s per side, burst 20; 60 s warm-up; held rejection;
- P3 uses NV forecasts (target y_k model, as E003) attached to the delivered view states.

| Variant | Rule |
|---|---|
| REF | clean view; must reproduce E003's P0 and P3[M2] rows exactly |
| N | degraded view; quotes on stale states and keeps stale orders resting |
| **U** | N, plus: at every decision, want no order on either side if α(v.t) ≥ τ (a causal check on v.state) |
| **P** | N, plus: want no order on either side while v.t ∈ W_P |

**Windows.**
- **W_U** = {t : α(t) ≥ τ}, restricted to post-warm-up episode time. Equivalently, W_U = ∪_j [t_exch(j) + τ,
  d(j+1) + md_delay), where d(j+1) is the episode end for the last state. It depends only on the degraded delivery
  stream, so it is known before simulation.
- **U's causal rule and W_U membership** are asserted to agree at every decision, ties included.
- **W_P** is built from the maximal spells of W_U on the linear post-warm-up span of each episode. Each spell is shifted
  circularly by ⌊u·L⌋, with u drawn from U[0.25, 0.75] (role 4). u is redrawn from the same deterministic stream if any
  image would be cut at the seam, or would abut another image across the seam.
  - The redraws are capped at 1,000. An episode that reaches the cap is excluded from both windows and counted.
  - The number of redraws is reported.
- **Matching.** W_P is then an *exactly duty-matched* placebo: equal total duration, and an equal spell multiset
  (asserted to the microsecond). Turnover, fills and two-sided presence are *not* matched. Their U/P ratios are reported,
  with the note "participation differs" outside [0.85, 1.15].

**Engine options** (both off by default, so E002 and E003 reproduce exactly):
1. `view`: a list of (d + md_delay, State copy) that drives participant decisions. Exchange events always use the true
   states. Each view state carries its source index, and its prices must equal the true state's (asserted).
2. `extra_decision_times`: re-decisions on the last view state through `redecide`. They do not clear held rejections.
   - They are placed at the start edges of W_U and at both edges of W_P, for U, P and N alike, so that re-decision
     opportunities are symmetric. W_U's end edges always coincide with a view delivery, so they need no extra decision.
   - At equal times, view-delivery decisions are processed before extra decisions and acknowledgements.
   - With empty windows, U = P = N bit-for-bit (asserted).

State lists are never mutated; their hashes are asserted unchanged across jobs.

## 5. Outcomes
- **Π5** is the fee-free 5-second mark-to-mid of fills: Π5 = Σ over fills of side × (mid_exch(t + 5 s) − price) × qty, in
  USDT. It uses exchange-side states. Fills whose t + 5 s lies beyond the episode end are excluded from both numerator and
  denominator, and counted.
- **Window quality.** For a set of participant windows W, define the exchange-clock windows W^x = W + entry latency (when
  cancels take effect). Then q(W) = 10⁴ · Σ_{fills ∈ W^x} Π5 / Σ_{fills ∈ W^x} notional, in bp, pooled over replicates
  within a session. A filled order is counted once per (replicate, order id).
- **Forecasts** (both targets), per session and cell:
  - R²_OS in percentage points;
  - IC, hit rate, calibration slope by bucket;
  - share of rows with |f| ≥ half spread;
  - realised degradation statistics.
- **Policies** as in E003: gross and net, gross bp of notional, markouts, presence, turnover, paced time, rejections,
  ES and ES per 1,000 USDT/h, |inventory| and drawdown. Net is reported at VIP0 and at LP tier 4 (hypothetical).

## 6. Pre-registered analysis (evaluation sessions s6…s15)

**Statistics.**
- The session is the unit.
- 10,000-draw paired session bootstrap (seed 20261024), percentile intervals. Paired t-intervals are reported as a
  sensitivity.
- Undefined sessions are excluded from both the bootstrap and the sign count. A contrast enters a decision only if it is
  defined in ≥ 8 sessions.
- Sign counts are given as "k of the defined sessions".

**Primary family: 2 decisions, H4-W[P0] and H4-W[P3].** Both use the D1-E cell, intermediate × regional, and the N run.
Labels use 95% intervals. Bonferroni 97.5% intervals are reported, and a label that changes under them is marked *not
robust to multiplicity*.

**Contrast.** D_W = q(W_P) − q(W_U), in bp. A positive value means fills in staleness-timed windows are worse than fills
in duty-matched, randomly timed windows: an uncertainty-aware rule that withdraws when its information is stale would
avoid worse fills than the same amount of withdrawal at random times.

**Margin.** δ = 1.0 bp, about one tick at the evaluation relative tick, and E002's materiality threshold.

**Definedness.** D_W is undefined in a session if either W_U^x or W_P^x has fewer than 20 distinct filled orders. The
withdrawn-share and overlap gates are computed per session, pooled over replicates.

**Labels:**
- **Stale-fill selection supported:** lower bound > δ, and D_W > 0 in ≥ 8 of the defined sessions.
- **Immaterial selection (< δ):** upper bound < δ, and D_W < δ in ≥ 8 of the defined sessions. The report states whether
  the interval excludes 0.
- **Inconclusive:** otherwise. The note *underpowered* is added if the interval is wider than 2δ.
- **Not testable**, if any of:
  - the withdrawn share of valid time (W_U) is < 0.5% (rule inactive);
  - fewer than 8 sessions are defined;
  - W_P overlaps W_U in more than 10% of W_P time.

**Natural-staleness qualifier (pre-registered consequence).** D_W is also computed on the W_U spells that overlap a
scheduled stall, together with their shifted images. If that stall-only estimate would give a different label under the
same rules, the H4-W label carries the qualifier *sensitive to natural staleness*.

Natural staleness is concentrated in 2 evaluation sessions (2026-01-01 and 2026-05-01). Every natural spell ends in the
burst of activity that produces the next update, which falls inside W_U^x. The qualifier exists to catch that effect.

A *supported* label is a statement about fill selection. A claim that the uncertainty-aware **policy** outperforms
also requires the realised U-vs-P comparison below.

**Ex-ante expectations.**
- **Sign:** D_W > 0 is expected *mechanically*. Stale resting orders get filled by trades that go through them at stale
  prices.
- **The open question is the size:** does the gap exceed δ = 1 bp?
- **The constant-delay evidence in section 0 is not evidence here.** The latency brackets also vary entry and
  acknowledgement latency, and they never freeze the view.
- **P3 specifically:** P3 already withdraws on its forecast, so its stale exposure is smaller.

**Power.** With n = 10, "supported" needs D_W ≳ δ + 1.0 σ_D for about 85–90% power (reviewer simulation of the decision
rule). The *underpowered* note (interval width > 2δ) is expected only if σ_D ≳ 1.6 bp.
- Under D1-E, W_U covers about 2.7% of time (the stalled share is 4.5%). Window fill counts are still in the hundreds to
  thousands per session, so σ_D mainly reflects heterogeneity between sessions.
- For scale, E003's whole-sample SDs are about 0.1 bp for paired 5-second markout differences (P3[M2] − P0) and about
  0.3 bp for gross bp differences (P3[M2] − P3[−M2]).

**Also reported:**
- the share of W_U time and of W_U^x fills that comes from spells with no stall;
- the share of W_P time and of W_P^x fills that lies inside any scheduled stall.

**Secondary decisions (95%; no family-wise claim).**
- **H4-F (forecast level)**, for D1-S and both targets, reported against the section 0 projections.
  - Contrasts: L = R²(REF) − R²(NV), A = R²(UA-λ) − R²(NV), B = R²(UA-λ) − R²(PL-λ), C = R²(RF-λ) − R²(NV).
  - ρ_A = ΣA/ΣL and ρ_B = ΣB/ΣL are bootstrapped jointly.
  - Labels, applied in order:
    1. *not testable* if mean L < 0.20 pp or L's interval includes 0;
    2. *supported* if ρ_A lower bound > 0.25 and ρ_B lower bound > 0.10;
    3. *recalibration only* if ρ_A lower bound > 0.25 and ρ_B upper bound < 0.10 (practically unreachable at D1-S,
       since ρ_B ≈ ρ_A(1 − p));
    4. *not supported* if ρ_A upper bound < 0.25 (flag *UA harmful* if A's upper bound < 0);
    5. *inconclusive* otherwise.
  - Expected: y_k *supported* (ρ_A ≈ 0.37); y_view *not supported* (ρ_A ≈ 0).
  - The informative quantity is the observed ρ_A minus its projection, reported per target.
  - The same labels are applied to D1-E, D3 and D4. D1-E is expected to be *not testable*, given its 4.5% stalled share.
  - D1-S1 and D1-S3 are descriptive.
  - D2, the negative control, is expected to be *not testable*. If it is labelled anything else, the forecast-level
    labels are flagged *pending review*.
- **H4-0 (do-no-harm on D0):** R²(UA-λ trained on D1-S) − R²(REF) on clean rows, for target y_k.
  - *Harmless* if the lower bound > −0.05 pp; *costly* if the upper bound < −0.05 pp; inconclusive otherwise.
  - Expected: harmless. At D0, nearly all rows fall in the fresh bucket, where λ ≈ 1.

**Descriptive (no labels).**
- **Realised U vs P runs (D1-E):** the gross bp of notional difference Δb, its value scaled by N's notional share in W_U^x
  (Δb/s), U/P participation ratios, Π5, and the avoided-loss rate. The unscaled Δb is diluted by the 4.5% window share,
  so it is not labelled.
- U vs N; P vs N.
- **Net per hour at VIP0,** with the fee-saving term (maker × Δ notional) separated out and labelled *mechanical*.
  LP tier 4 (hypothetical).
- The conservative and optimistic fill models (1 replicate).
- D1-S at the policy level.
- P3 with UA-λ vs PL-λ forecasts, labelled *participation-changing; descriptive*. Shrinkage makes P3 quote both sides
  more during staleness, so a forecast-level result is not a policy result.
- UA-β; transfer across severities; the reference false-trigger share; the D2 flag trigger share under D0.

**No label rewards abstention.** No decision uses net per hour.

## 7. Acceptance criteria before the run
1. **Reproduction.** Engine options are off by default. E002 tables reproduce bit-for-bit (15 sessions). Under D0:
   - j(k) = k;
   - NV = REF bitwise;
   - REF forecasts equal E003 per row and per aggregate (R²_OS, IC, hit rate and share columns);
   - REF policy rows equal E003's frozen rows.
2. **Tests:**
   - poison test (withheld states, trades and ids, and true sizes set to garbage in the participant arrays);
   - causality (perturbing anything delivered after t_k − md_delay, or any target);
   - exchange-stream hash invariance;
   - a scripted policy driven only by fixed extra decision times, with never-rejected prices, has identical fills under
     clean and degraded views;
   - view prices equal true prices;
   - seed determinism and role independence;
   - realised shares as designed (D1-S 16.7% ± 1 pp, D1-E 4.5% ± 0.5 pp, D2 14% ± 1 pp, D4 25% ± 2 pp over 10⁶ s);
   - noise properties;
   - D3 FIFO;
   - walk-forward and training-role use only;
   - λ recovery on a known AR process, for both targets;
   - bucket pooling;
   - W_U and W_P: equal duration, equal spell multiset, the seam rule, and U's causal rule = W_U membership;
   - empty windows ⇒ U = P = N bitwise;
   - no mutation of State lists;
   - every label on synthetic tables, including the undefined-session and sign-count rules.
3. **Freeze.** This specification, the configuration and the τ timing artifact are committed before any E004 outcome,
   after an independent review with no unresolved critical or major finding. The schedule-hash list is committed before
   the code review. The adversarial code review must likewise leave no unresolved critical or major finding.
4. **Runner guards:**
   - clean tree;
   - provenance before and after;
   - input hashes, including the Tardis trade files;
   - refuses test sessions, an uncommitted specification and a non-empty output directory;
   - E003's run-time invariants;
   - a status column on every output row.

## 8. Forward set for the single final held-out evaluation (E004 selects nothing)

The final held-out run is governed by a separate protocol, committed before the test sessions are opened. From E004 it
forwards, unchanged:
- **Models:** REF, NV, RF-λ, PL-λ, UA-λ and UA-β. NV uses E003 §10's all-15-session M2 with its clip bounds, and λ is
  fitted once on all 15 discovery sessions with training seeds. Nothing is refitted on test data.
- **Cells:** every cell of section 2.
- **Policies:** P0 and P3[M2] in the REF, N, U and P variants under D1-E (D1-S descriptive).
- **Seeds:** test-day schedules are fixed by the committed hashes; test replicates = 3.

**Confirmation** is a point-estimate check on 5 sessions. The gates are applied first, in their test-session form; a
decision whose gates fail is *not confirmable*.
- **H4-W gates:** withdrawn share ≥ 0.5%; at least 20 distinct filled orders in both windows in ≥ 4 of 5 sessions; D_W
  defined in ≥ 4 of 5 sessions; overlap ≤ 10%.
- **H4-F gates:** mean L ≥ 0.20 pp and L > 0 in ≥ 4 of 5 sessions.
- H4-0 and the descriptive results are reported only.

For every non-inconclusive discovery label, the same-side check applies:
- **H4-W supported:** D_W > δ on the mean and > 0 in ≥ 4 of 5 sessions.
- **H4-W immaterial:** D_W < δ on the mean and in ≥ 4 of 5 sessions.
- **H4-F supported:** ρ_A > 0.25 and ρ_B > 0.10, with B > 0 in ≥ 4 of 5 sessions.
- **H4-F not supported:** ρ_A < 0.25.

## 9. Known limitations
- Degradations are synthetic and their rates are stress levels. D1-E's durations are empirical, but its rate is about
  7,000× observed.
- Stalls are assumed independent of the market.
- Indicators assume clock sync and an honest flag. Order entry and acknowledgements are assumed unaffected.
- W_U includes natural staleness between depth updates, hence the pre-registered stall-only qualifier.
- Forecast-level results are largely implied by the target choice and E003's horizon profile (section 0). UA-λ only
  rescales.
- The base signal is weak.
- The analysis is not blind. There are 10 sessions; percentile intervals can under-cover. Window quality rests on modest
  fill counts.
- All E002 and E003 limitations apply.
